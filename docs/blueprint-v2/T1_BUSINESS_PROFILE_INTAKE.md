# Tranche 1 E01 — governed structured business discovery

Manual, compact business profile intake is persisted as one immutable source in the existing knowledge document/chunk owner. A separate active administrator reviews it; explicit expiry, HTTPS source reference, digest, bounded structured fields and designated canonical agent scopes are enforced. The existing knowledge studio displays current reviewed or quarantined snapshots and reuses revoke/scrub, review, audit and tenant isolation. This is not a second legal identity or business master, verified factual claims, automatic website acquisition, or production acceptance. Unit and actual PostgreSQL scenarios cover idempotency, spoofing, maker/checker, expiry, ACL and withdrawal. General authorized extraction, source verification, full business-profile revision selection and customer acceptance remain.
